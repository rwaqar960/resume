About

The layer most Flutter developers don't touch

I'm technical lead for mobile across four healthcare products at CDOXS — leading 3–4 engineers while personally owning the Flutter codebase. Architecture, review standards, and release process are mine; so is the code.

Most cross-platform work stops at the framework. Mine hasn't: I wrote the capture and filter engines for ScanLeap natively in Kotlin and Swift behind a single Dart interface, because no existing plugin met the accuracy bar. And I built the pipeline behind ClinixSummary.ai end to end — audio capture through transcription, structured clinical extraction, and clinician sign-off — in a domain where a hallucinated line in a patient note is a safety event, not a bug.

The parts I'm most proud of are the ones the team kept using after I moved on: the shared calling, chat, and document-detection components, and the delivery process now running on every major project.
